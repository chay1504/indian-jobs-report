India Job Market Visualizer Read the report ↓ Based on karpathy/jobs

This is an India version of Andrej Karpathy's US Job Market Visualizer. It shows 127 occupations (every NCO-2015 occupation group that appears in the data) covering — workers aged 15+, built from the unit-level records of MoSPI's Periodic Labour Force Survey, January–December 2025 (11.5 lakh people surveyed). Each rectangle's area is proportional to the number of workers. Color shows the selected layer. Hover or tap a tile for details.

What changed for India: India has no equivalent of the BLS ten-year outlook, so that layer is replaced by Formal jobs (share of workers who are regular salaried employees with social security). A new Income tax layer shows the share of each occupation's workers who earn above today's ₹12 lakh zero-tax line, and the report below estimates how much of India's income tax each occupation pays. The Digital AI Exposure layer uses Karpathy's scoring prompt word for word, run twice independently per occupation and averaged.

View the Digital AI Exposure scoring prompt (Karpathy's, source sentence adapted)

Caveat on Digital AI Exposure scores: these are rough LLM estimates, not predictions. A high score does not mean the job will disappear; demand for software, analysis or teaching could grow as each worker becomes more productive. Scores describe how digital the work is, and do not account for demand elasticity, regulation, cost of adoption in India, or social preference for human workers.

Who pays: the occupations behind India's income tax

Estimated share of individual income tax under the FY 2025-26 rules, by occupation. The estimate takes CBDT's actual distribution of returns by income (AY 2023-24) and splits each income band between occupations using PLFS 2025: salary income goes by the occupations of salaried high earners, and business and professional income by the occupations of self-employed high earners.
