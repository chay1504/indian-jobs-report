# Vehicle, Window, Laundry and Other Hand Cleaning Workers

NCO-2015 group 912. Category: Cleaners, helpers & elementary.

## What they do

Vehicle, Window, Laundry and Other Hand Cleaning Workers clean windows, showcases or other surfaces of buildings or vehicles and press, launder or dry-clean linen and other textiles by hand.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Hand Launderers and Pressers** (9121): Hand launderers and pressers launder, press or dry-clean garments, linen and other textiles by hand.
- **Vehicle Cleaners** (9122): Vehicle cleaners, wash, clean and polish vehicles.
- **Window Cleaners** (9123): Window Cleaners wash and polish windows and other glass fittings.
- **Other Cleaning Workers** (9129): Other Cleaning Workers clean surface, materials and objects, such as carpets, walls, swimming pools and cooling towers, using specialized cleaning equipment and chemicals.

Dhobi, Laundry Workers (Marker, Sorter), Dry Cleaner, Hand, Spot Remover, Rug Cleaner, Presser, Hand, Dry Cleaner, Spot, Launderers, Dry Cleaners and Pressers, Other, Cleaner, Window, Washer, Driving Assistant, Vehicle, Window and Related Cleaners, Other

- Workers: about 11.3 lakh (PLFS sample of 939 people)

- Women: 31%; urban: 61%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 3%

- Main industries: Other personal services (laundry, beauty, funeral), Motor vehicle trade & repair, Land transport
