# Food Processing and Related Trade Workers

NCO-2015 group 751. Category: Crafts, food processing & garments.

## What they do

Food processing and related trades workers slaughter animals, treat and prepare them and related food items for human and animal consumptions, make various kinds of bread, cakes and other flour products, process and preserve fruit, vegetables and related foods, taste and grade various food products and beverages, or prepare tobacco and make tobacco products.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Butchers, Fishmongers and Related Food Preparers** (7511): Butchers, fishmongers and related food preparers slaughter animals, clean, cut and dress meat and fish, remove bones and prepare related food items or preserve meat, fish and other foods and food products by drying, salting or smoking.
- **Bakers, Pastry-Cooks and Confectionery Makers** (7512): Bakers, pastry-cooks and confectionery makers make various kinds of bread, cakes, and other flour products, as well as handmade chocolate and sugar confectionery.
- **Dairy Products Makers** (7513): Dairy products makers process butter and various types of cheese, cream or other dairy products.
- **Fruits, Vegetables and Related Preservers** (7514): Fruit, vegetable and related preservers process or preserve fruit, nuts and related foods in various ways including cooking, drying, salting, or juice or oil extraction.
- **Food and Beverage Tasters and Graders** (7515): Food and beverage tasters and graders inspect, taste and grade various types of agricultural products, food and beverages.
- **Tobacco Preparers and Tobacco Products Makers** (7516): Tobacco preparers and tobacco products makers prepare tobacco leaves and make various tobacco products.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 65.8 lakh (PLFS sample of 4,923 people)
- Employment type: 79% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 14% regular salaried, 7% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 2%
- Government/public sector employer: 0%
- Women: 64%; urban: 33%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 5%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 57,600
- Main industries: Tobacco products, Food products, Food & beverage service
