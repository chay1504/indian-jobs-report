# Heavy Truck and Bus Drivers

NCO-2015 group 833. Category: Drivers & transport.

## What they do

Heavy Truck and Bus Drivers drive and tend heavy trucks, Lorries, buses or street tramcars to transport goods, liquids, heavy materials, mail or passengers.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Bus and Tram Drivers** (8331): Bus and tram drivers drive and tend buses or street tramcars to transport passengers, mail or goods.
- **Heavy Truck and Lorry Drivers** (8332): Heavy-truck and lorry drivers drive and tend heavy motor vehicles to transport goods, liquids and heavy materials over short or long distances.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Tram Car Driver, Trolley Driver, Driver, Bus, Commercial Vehicle Driver, Commercial Vehicle Driver- Assistant, Driver, Fire Brigade Vehicles, Driver Road Test, Test Driver, Signalman, Tram, Bus and Tram Drivers, Other, Driver Truck, Tram Car Driver (Mines), Heavy Truck and Lorry Drivers, Other
