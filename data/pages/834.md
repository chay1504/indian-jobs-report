# Mobile Plant Operators

NCO-2015 group 834. Category: Drivers & transport.

## What they do

Mobile Plant Operators drive, tend, operate and monitor special-purpose motorised, machinery or equipment used for clearing or preparing land; digging, moving, and spreading earth, rock and similar materials; and lifting or moving heavy objects.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operators** (8341): Mobile Farm and Forestry Plant Operators drive, tend, operate and monitor one or more types of special-purpose motorised, mobile machinery or equipment used in agricultural, horticultural and forestry operations.
- **Earth Moving and Related Plant Operators** (8342): Earth Moving and Related Plant Operators operate machines to excavate, grade, level, smooth and compact earth or similar materials.
- **Crane, Hoist and Related Plant Operators** (8343): Crane, Hoist and Related Plant Operators operate and monitor stationary and mobile cranes and other hoisting equipment.
- **Lifting Truck Operators** (8344): Lifting-Truck Operators drive, operate and monitor lifting-truck or similar vehicle to transport, lift and stack pallets with goods.
