# Building Frames and Related Trades Workers

NCO-2015 group 711. Category: Construction trades.

## What they do

Building frame and related trades workers construct, maintain and repair buildings, erect and repair foundations, walls and structures of brick, stone and similar materials, shape and finish stone for building and other purposes, and perform miscellaneous construction and building maintenance tasks

## Occupation families in this group

- **House Builders** (7111): House builders erect, maintain and repair houses and similar small buildings using either traditional or modern techniques and materials.
- **Bricklayers and Related Workers** (7112): Bricklayers and related workers lay bricks, pre-cut stones and other types of building blocks in mortar to construct and repair walls, partitions, arches and other structures.
- **Stonemasons, Stone Cutters, Splitters and Carvers** (7113): Stonemasons, stone cutters, splitters and carvers cut and shape hard and soft stone blocks and slabs for the construction and maintenance of stone structures and monumental masonry, and carve designs and figures in stone.
- **Concrete Placers, Concrete Finishers and Related Workers** (7114): Concrete placers, concrete finishers and related workers erect reinforced concrete frameworks and structures, make forms for moulding concrete, reinforce concrete surfaces, cement openings in walls or casings for wells, finish and repair cement surfaces and carry out terrazzo work.
- **Carpenters and Joiners** (7115): Carpenters and joiners cut, shape, assemble, erect, maintain and repair various types of structures and fittings made from wood and other materials.
- **Building Frame and Related Traders Workers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (7119): This family covers building frame and related trades workers not classified elsewhere in group 711, Building frame and related trades workers. For instance, the group includes steeplejacks, scaffolders and demolition workers.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 1.10 crore (PLFS sample of 8,283 people)
- Employment type: 42% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 9% regular salaried, 49% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 1%
- Government/public sector employer: 0%
- Women: 5%; urban: 25%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 3%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Construction of buildings, Furniture, Specialised construction
