# Sports and Fitness Workers

NCO-2015 group 342. Category: Legal, social, arts & media.

## What they do

Sports and Fitness Workers prepare for and compete in sporting events for financial gain, train amateur and professional sportsmen and women to enhance performance, promote participation and standards in sport, organize and officiate sporting events, and provide instruction, training and supervision for various forms of exercise and other recreational activities.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Athletes and Sports Players** (3421): Athletes and sport players participate in competitive sporting events. They train and compete, either individually or as part of a team, in their chosen sport.
- **Sports Coaches, Instructors and Officials** (3422): Sports coaches, instructors and officials work with amateur and professional sportspersons to enhance performance, encourage greater participation in sport, and organize and officiate in sporting events according to established rules.
- **Fitness and Recreation Instructors, and Programme Leaders** (3423): Fitness and Recreation Instructors and Programme leaders lead, guide and instruct groups and individuals in recreational, fitness or outdoor adventure activities.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Professional Sportsman/Professional, Athlete, Jockey, Athletic Coach, Sports Coach, Riding Instructor, Umpire, Marker/Groundsmen and Women, Sports Coaches, Instructors and Officials, Other, Fitness Trainer/Fitness Instructor, Physical Training Instructor/Physical Education Teacher, Lifeguard, Fitness and Recreation Instructors and Programme Leaders

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 3.1 lakh (PLFS sample of 274 people)
- Employment type: 37% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 62% regular salaried, 1% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 17%
- Government/public sector employer: 15%
- Women: 18%; urban: 67%
- Typical education (median worker): Graduate; graduate or above: 51%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 234,000
- Main industries: Sports & recreation, Education, Human health
