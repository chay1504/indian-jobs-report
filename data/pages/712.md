# Building Finishers and Related Trades Workers

NCO-2015 group 712. Category: Construction trades.

## What they do

Building finishers and related trades workers cover, apply or install, maintain and repair roofs, floors, walls, insulation systems, glass in windows or other frames, as well as plumbing, piping and electrical systems in buildings and other structures.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Roofers** (7121): Roofers build and repair roofs on all types of buildings using one or more kinds of materials.
- **Floor Layers and Tile Setters** (7122): Floor layers and tile setters install, maintain and repair flooring, and cover floors, walls and other surfaces with tiles or mosaic panels for decorative or other purposes.
- **Plasterers** (7123): Plasterers install, maintain and repair plasterboard in buildings, and apply decorative and protective coverings of plaster, cement and similar material to the interiors and exteriors of structures.
- **Insulation Workers** (7124): Insulation workers apply and repair insulating materials to buildings, boilers, pipes or refrigeration and air-conditioning equipment.
- **Glaziers** (7125): Glaziers measure, cut, finish, fit and install flat glass and mirrors.
- **Plumbers and Pipe Fitters** (7126): Plumbers and pipe fitters assemble, install, repair and maintain pipe systems, fittings and fixtures for water, gas, drainage, sewerage systems, and hydraulic and pneumatic equipment.
- **Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Mechanics** (7127): Air conditioning and refrigeration mechanics assemble, install, maintain and repair air conditioning and refrigeration systems and equipment.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 46.5 lakh (PLFS sample of 3,580 people)
- Employment type: 47% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 10% regular salaried, 43% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 3%
- Government/public sector employer: 3%
- Women: 5%; urban: 33%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 5%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 186,000
- Main industries: Construction of buildings, Specialised construction, Repair of computers & household goods
