# Agricultural, Forestry and Fishery Labourers

NCO-2015 group 921. Category: Agriculture, forestry & fishing.

## What they do

Agricultural, Forestry and Fishery Labourers perform simple and routine tasks in the production of crops and livestock, cultivation and maintenance of gardens and parks, exploitation and conservation of forests, and conduct aquaculture and fisheries operations.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Crop Farm Labourers** (9211): Crop farm labourers perform simple and routine tasks in the production of crops such as fruit, nuts, grains and vegetables on farms.
- **Livestock Farm Labourers** (9212): Livestock Farm Labourers perform simple and routine tasks in farm production of animals, including poultry and insects.
- **Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourers** (9213): Mixed Crop and Livestock Farm Labourers perform simple and routine tasks in farm production of both crops and animals.
- **Garden and Horticultural Labourers** (9214): Garden and Horticultural Labourers perform simple and routine tasks in operations to cultivate and maintain trees, shrubs, flowers and other plants in parks and private gardens, to produce saplings, bulbs and seeds, or grow vegetables and flowers by intensive cultivation techniques.
- **Forestry Labourers** (9215): Forestry Labourers perform simple and routine tasks to cultivate and maintain natural and plantation forests, and logging, felling and sawing trees.
- **Fishery and Aquaculture Labourers** (9216): Fishery and Aquaculture Labourers, perform simple and routine tasks to cultivate, catch and harvest fish and seafood in aquaculture and inland, coastal and deep sea fishing operations.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Ploughman, Labourer, Agriculture, Picker (Tobacco), Farm Hands and Labourers, Other, Labourer, Plantation, Tappers, Plantation Labourers and Related Workers, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 4.80 crore (PLFS sample of 27,954 people)
- Employment type: 2% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 3% regular salaried, 95% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 1%
- Government/public sector employer: 3%
- Women: 53%; urban: 6%
- Typical education (median worker): Below primary; graduate or above: 1%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 82,800
- Main industries: Crop & animal production, Civil engineering, Fishing & aquaculture
