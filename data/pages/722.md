# Blacksmiths, Tool Makers and Related Trades Workers

NCO-2015 group 722. Category: Metal, machinery & electrical trades.

## What they do

Blacksmiths, Toolmakers and Related Trades Workers, hammer and forge bars, rods or ingots of iron, steel and other metals to make and repair various kinds of tools, equipment and other articles, set machine tools for operators or set and operate various machine tools, and polish and sharpen surfaces.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Blacksmiths, Hammersmiths and Forging Press Workers** (7221): Blacksmiths, Hammersmiths and forging press workers hammer and forge bars, rods, ingots and plates of iron, steel or other metals, and draw wire to make and repair various kinds of tools, metal articles, pieces of equipment, and agricultural and related implements.
- **Tool Makers and Related Workers** (7222): Toolmakers and related workers make and repair custom made and specialized tools, sports guns, locks, dies, patterns machinery components and other metal articles using hand and machine tools to work metal to fine tolerances.
- **Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators** (7223): Metal Working Machine Tool Setters and Operators set and/or operate, various machine tools working to fine tolerances.
- **Metal Polishers, Wheel Grinders and Tool Sharpeners** (7224): Metal polishers, wheel grinders and tool sharpeners polish and grind metal surfaces and sharpen tools.

- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 19%
- Government/public sector employer: 1%
- Women: 13%; urban: 48%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 10%

- Main industries: Fabricated metal products, Basic metals, Motor vehicles
