# Client Information Workers

NCO-2015 group 422. Category: Office & admin support.

## What they do

Client Information Clerks provide or obtain information in person, by telephone or electronic means such as e-mail in connection with making travel arrangements, describing the products or services of an organization, registering and greeting guests and visitors, making appointments, connecting telephone calls and collecting information from survey respondents or applicants for services.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Travel Consultants and Clerks** (4221): Travel Consultants and Clerks supply information, arrange travel itineraries, obtain travel and accommodation reservations and organize group tours.
- **Contact Centre Information Clerks** (4222): Contact Centre Information Clerks provide advice and information to clients, respond to queries regarding a company’s or an organization’s goods, services or policies, and process financial transactions using the telephone or electronic communications media, such as email. - **Telephone Switch Board Operators** (4223): Telephone Switch Board Operators operate telephone communications switchboards and consoles to establish telephone connections, receive caller inquiries and service problem reports, and record and relay messages to staff or clients.
- **Hotel Receptionists** (4224): Hotel receptionists register guests, assign rooms, issue keys, provide information concerning the hotel services, make room reservations, keep a record of rooms available for occupancy and present statements of charges to departing guests and receive payment.
- **Inquiry Clerks** (4225): Inquiry Clerks respond to personal, written, electronic mail, and telephone enquiries and complaints about the organization’s goods, services and policies, provide information and refer people to other sources. They are employed in locations which put them in direct contact with clients or with the production of the goods and services provided.
- **Receptionist (General)** (4226): Receptionists (General) receive and welcome visitors, clients, or guests and respond to enquiries and requests including arranging for appointments.
- **Survey and Market Research Interviewers** (4227): Survey and Market Research Interviewers interview people and record their responses to survey and market research questions on a range of topics.
- **Client Information Worker, Not Elsewhere Classified** (4229): This unit group includes Client Information Workers not included elsewhere in Group 422, Client Information Workers. For instance, it includes workers who obtain and process information from clients needed to determine eligibility for services.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 8.0 lakh (PLFS sample of 683 people)
- Employment type: 6% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 93% regular salaried, 1% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 47%
- Government/public sector employer: 9%
- Women: 54%; urban: 78%
- Typical education (median worker): Graduate; graduate or above: 54%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Human health, Office admin & business support (incl. BPO/call centres), Computer programming & IT consultancy
