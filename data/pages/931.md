# Mining and Construction Labourers

NCO-2015 group 931. Category: Labourers (construction, factory, transport).

## What they do

Mining and construction labourers perform simple and routine manual tasks in mining, quarrying, civil engineering and building operations.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Mining and Quarrying Labourers** (9311): Mining and Quarrying Labourers perform routine tasks in mining and quarrying operations.
- **Civil Engineering Labourers** (9312): Civil Engineering Labourers perform routine tasks in connection with the building and maintenance of roads, railways, dams and other civil engineering projects.
- **Building Construction Labourers** (9313): Building Construction Labourers perform routine tasks in connection with building construction and demolition work.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Mines, Quarrymen, Related Workers, Mazdoor/Helper, Digger (Mine and Quarry), Mining and Quarrying Labourers, Other, Labourer, Other, Track Repairer (Rail Transport), Construction and Maintenance Labourers, Other, Well Digger Helper, Helper Bar Bender and Fixer, Helper Mason, Helper Carpenter-Shuttering and Scaffolding, Helper Electrician, Helper Laboratory and Field Technician, Helper Painter and Decorator, Building Construction Labourers, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 5.06 crore (PLFS sample of 34,443 people)
- Employment type: 1% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 2% regular salaried, 96% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 1%
- Government/public sector employer: 8%
- Women: 17%; urban: 18%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 2%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 123,429
- Main industries: Construction of buildings, Civil engineering, Specialised construction
