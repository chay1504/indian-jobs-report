# Printing Trades Workers

NCO-2015 group 732. Category: Crafts, food processing & garments.

## What they do

Printing Trades workers compose and set type prior to printing, set up and operate printing presses, bind and finish printed products, and prepare stencils and operate screen printing equipment

## Occupation families in this group

- **Pre-press Technicians** (7321): Pre-press Technicians proof, format, set and compose text and graphics into a form suitable for use in various printing processes and representation in other visual media.
- **Printers** (7322): Printers set up and operate digital, letterpress, lithographic, flexographic, gravure, newspaper and other printing presses.
- **Print Finishing and Binding Workers** (7323): Print finishing and binding workers bind books and other publications, and finish printed products by hand or machine.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 5.5 lakh (PLFS sample of 529 people)
- Employment type: 38% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 58% regular salaried, 4% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 17%
- Government/public sector employer: 4%
- Women: 8%; urban: 76%
- Typical education (median worker): Secondary (Class 10); graduate or above: 25%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 192,000
- Main industries: Printing, Office admin & business support (incl. BPO/call centres), Other professional & technical
