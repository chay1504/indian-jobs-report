# Electrical Equipment Installers and Repairers

NCO-2015 group 741. Category: Metal, machinery & electrical trades.

## What they do

Electrical equipment installers and repairers install, fit and maintain electrical wiring systems and related equipment, electrical machinery and other electrical apparatus and electrical transmission and supply lines and cables.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Building and Related Electricians** (7411): Building and related electricians install, maintain and repair electrical wiring systems and related equipment and fixtures.
- **Electrical Mechanics and Fitters** (7412): Electrical mechanics and fitters fit, adjust, install and repair electrical machinery and other electrical apparatus and equipment in buildings, factories, workshops, or other places.
- **Electrical Line Installers and Repairers** (7413): Electrical Line Installers and Repairers install, repair and join electrical transmission and supply cables and related equipment.
- **Electrical and Electronic Equipment Mechanics and Fitters and Related Workers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (7419): This Family covers workers not elsewhere classified in the Group 724, Electrical and electronic equipment mechanics and fitters. For instance, occupations involving inspection of television receivers during final assembly, checking of telephone and telegraph equipment, doing armature and coil winding, repairing and charging battery, replacing blown out fuses etc.
