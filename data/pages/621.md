# Forestry and Related Workers

NCO-2015 group 621. Category: Agriculture, forestry & fishing.

## What they do

Forestry and related workers plan, organize and perform operations to cultivate, conserve and exploit natural and plantation forests.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Forestry and Related Workers** (6210): Forestry and related workers plan, organize and perform operations to cultivate, conserve and exploit natural and plantation forests.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 9.3 lakh (PLFS sample of 691 people)
- Employment type: 44% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 22% regular salaried, 34% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 16%
- Government/public sector employer: 12%
- Women: 31%; urban: 13%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 6%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 108,000
- Main industries: Forestry & logging, Crop & animal production, Wood products
