# Manufacturing Labourers

NCO-2015 group 932. Category: Labourers (construction, factory, transport).

## What they do

Manufacturing Labourers perform a variety of manual tasks in manufacturing, assist the work of Machine Operators and assemblers, and undertake product-sorting and hand-assembling of components.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Hand Packers** (9321): Hand Packers weigh, pack and label materials and products by hand.
- **Manufacturing Labourers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (9329): Manufacturing Labourers not elsewhere classified assist the work of Machine Operators and Assemblers and perform a variety of manual tasks in manufacturing, excluding packing and labelling finished products.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Packer, Hand, Packager and Despatcher, Labeller, Tagger and Labeller, Stenciler, Stamper, Hand, Weigher, Issuing and Return In charge, Diamond Bagger and Flueter, Counter, Container Washer, Machine, Machining Assistant or Helper, Hand Packers, Others, Khatedar, Salt Production, Mukadam, Salt Production, Salt Reclamation Worker, Checker, Tester, Sorter, Accessory Fitter, Parts Picker

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 1.23 crore (PLFS sample of 8,884 people)
- Employment type: 6% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 64% regular salaried, 29% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 26%
- Government/public sector employer: 2%
- Women: 27%; urban: 45%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 4%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 144,000
- Main industries: Non-metallic mineral products (bricks, cement, glass), Food products, Basic metals
