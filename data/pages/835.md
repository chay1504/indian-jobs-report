# Ships’ Deck Crews and Related Workers

NCO-2015 group 835. Category: Drivers & transport.

## What they do

Ship’s Deck Crews and Related workers carry out deck duties on board ship and similar duties on board other water-borne craft.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Ships’ Deck Crews and Related Workers** (8350): Ship’s Deck Crews and Related Workers carry out deck duties on board ship and similar duties on board other water-borne craft.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Serang, Deck/Bosun, Seaconny/OS (Ordinary Seaman), Lascar/OS (Ordinary Seaman), Driver, Launch/Tug Master, Boatman, Rudderman, Oarsman, Ships’ Deck Ratings, Barge Crews and Boatmen, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 0.9 lakh (PLFS sample of 90 people)
- Employment type: 60% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 31% regular salaried, 9% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 22%
- Government/public sector employer: 6%
- Women: 13%; urban: 34%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 11%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 360,000
- Main industries: Water transport, Warehousing & transport support, Motor vehicle trade & repair
