# Building and Housekeeping Supervisors

NCO-2015 group 515. Category: Personal services.

## What they do

Building and housekeeping supervisors co-ordinate, schedule, and supervise the work of cleaners and other housekeeping staff in commercial, industrial and residential premises. They take responsibility for housekeeping and caretaking functions in hotels, offices, apartments, houses and private dwellings.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisors in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments** (5151): Cleaning and Housekeeping Supervisors in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments organize, supervise and carry out housekeeping functions in order to keep clean and tidy the interiors, fixtures and facilities in these establishments.
- **Domestic Housekeepers** (5152): Domestic Housekeepers organize, supervise and carry out housekeeping functions in private households with or without the support of subordinate staff.
- **Building Caretakers** (5153): Building Caretakers take care of apartment houses, hotels, offices, churches and other buildings and maintain them and associated grounds in a clean and orderly condition. They may supervise other workers and contractors depending on the size and nature of the building concerned.
