# Locomotive Engine Drivers and Related Workers

NCO-2015 group 831. Category: Drivers & transport.

## What they do

Locomotive engine Drivers and Related Workers drive, or assist in driving, locomotive engines to transport passengers and freight, take charge of and safeguard railway freight trains during runs, control the movement of railway traffic by operating signals, switch rolling stock and clean trains in railway yards, prepare trains for hauling in mines and control their movement.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Locomotive Engine Drivers** (8311): Locomotive-Engine Drivers drive, or assist in driving, locomotive engines to transport passengers and freight.
- **Railway Braker, Signal and Switch Operators** (8312): Railway Brakers, Signallers and Shunters take charge of and safeguard railway freight trains during runs, control the movement of railway traffic by operating signals, switch rolling stock and clean trains in railway yards, prepare trains for hauling in mines and control their movement.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 2.9 lakh (PLFS sample of 265 people)
- Employment type: 26% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 71% regular salaried, 2% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 62%
- Government/public sector employer: 53%
- Women: 7%; urban: 57%
- Typical education (median worker): Higher secondary (Class 12); graduate or above: 35%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 408,000
- Main industries: Land transport, Specialised construction, Warehousing & transport support
