# Software and Application Developers, and Analysts

NCO-2015 group 251. Category: Computer & IT.

## What they do

Software and Application Developers and Analysts conduct research, plan, design, write, test, provide advice on and improve information technology systems, such as hardware, software and other applications to meet specific requirements.

## Occupation families in this group

- **System Analysts** (2511): Systems Analysts conduct research, analyse and evaluate client information technology requirements, procedures or problems, and develop and implement proposals, recommendations, and plans to improve current or future information systems.
- **Software Developers** (2512): Software Developers research, analyse and evaluate requirements for existing or new software applications and operating systems, and design, develop, test and maintain software solutions to meet these requirements.
- **Web and Multimedia Developers** (2513): Web and Multimedia Development Professionals combine design and technical knowledge to research, analyse, evaluate, design, program and modify websites, and applications that draw together text, graphics, animations, imaging, audio and video displays, and other interactive media.
- **Application Programmers** (2514): Application Programmers write and maintain programmable code outlined in technical instructions and specifications for software applications and operating systems.
- **Software and Application Developers and Analysts, Not Elsewhere Classified** (2519): This unit group covers software and applications developers and analysts not classified elsewhere in minor group 251, Software and applications developers and analysts. For instance, the group includes those professionals specialising in quality assurance including software testing.
