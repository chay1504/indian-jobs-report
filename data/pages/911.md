# Domestic, Hotel and Office Cleaners and Helpers

NCO-2015 group 911. Category: Cleaners, helpers & elementary.

## What they do

Domestic, Hotel and Office Cleaners and Helpers sweep, vacuum clean, wash, polish, take care of household linen, purchase household supplies; perform various tasks in order to keep clean and tidy the interiors and fixtures of hotels, offices and other establishments, as well as of aircraft, trains, buses and similar vehicles.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Domestic Cleaners and Helpers** (9111): Domestic Cleaners and Helpers sweep, vacuum clean, wash and polish, take care of household linen, purchase household supplies, prepare food, serve meals and perform various other domestic duties.
- **Cleaners and Helpers in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments** (9112): Cleaners and Helpers in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments perform various cleaning tasks in order to keep clean and tidy the interiors and fixtures of hotels, offices and other establishments, as well as of aircraft, trains, buses and similar vehicles.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Domestic Servant, Char-Dust Cleaner and Salvager, Cleaner, Home Restoration Service, Domestic and Related Helpers, Cleaners and Launderers, Other, Dusting Man, Office; Farash, Office/House Keeping Attendant, Cleaner Hospital, Helpers and Cleaners in Offices, Hotels and Other Establishments, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 98.4 lakh (PLFS sample of 7,035 people)
- Employment type: 4% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 88% regular salaried, 8% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 13%
- Government/public sector employer: 11%
- Women: 72%; urban: 66%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 2%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 96,000
- Main industries: Households as employers of domestic workers, Other personal services (laundry, beauty, funeral), Food & beverage service
