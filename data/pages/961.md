# Refuse Workers

NCO-2015 group 961. Category: Cleaners, helpers & elementary.

## What they do

Refuse workers collect, process and recycle garbage from buildings, yards, streets and other public places, or keep streets and other public places clean.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Garbage and Recycling Collectors** (9611): Garbage collectors collect and remove rubbish and items for recycling from buildings, yards, streets and other places.
- **Refuse Sorters** (9612): Refuse Sorters identify, collect and sort discarded items suitable for recycling at dump sites and recycling enterprises or in buildings, streets and other public places.
- **Sweepers and Related Labourers** (9613): Sweepers and related labourers sweep and clean streets, parks, airports, stations and other public places.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Garbage Collector (Motor Transport), E-Waste Collector, Garbage Collectors, Other, Sweeper, Dry, Sweeper, Wet, Sweeper, Sewer, Sweepers and Related Labourers, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 25.6 lakh (PLFS sample of 2,003 people)
- Employment type: 24% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 68% regular salaried, 8% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 31%
- Government/public sector employer: 42%
- Women: 36%; urban: 62%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 2%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 120,000
- Main industries: Waste collection & recovery, Public administration & defence, Education
