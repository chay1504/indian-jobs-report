# Tellers, Money Collectors and Related Clerks

NCO-2015 group 421. Category: Office & admin support.

## What they do

Tellers, Money Collectors and Related Clerks perform money-handling operations in establishments relating to banking, postal services, betting or gambling, pawning and debt-collecting.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Bank Tellers and Related Clerks** (4211): Bank Tellers and Related Clerks deal directly with clients of banks or post offices in connection with receiving, changing and paying out money, or providing mail services.
- **Bookmakers, Croupiers and Related Gaming Workers** (4212): Bookmakers and croupiers and related gambling workers determine odds and receive and pay off bets on results of sporting or other events, or conduct games of chance in gambling establishments.
- **Pawnbrokers and Money-lenders** (4213): Pawnbrokers and money-lenders lend money against articles deposited as pledges, or against property or other security.
- **Debt Collectors and Related Workers** (4214): Debt Collectors and Related Workers collect payments on overdue accounts and bad cheques and collect charity payments.
