# Life Science Professionals

NCO-2015 group 213. Category: Science & engineering.

## What they do

Life Science Professionals apply knowledge gained from research into human, animal and plant life and their interactions with each other and the environment to develop new knowledge, improve agricultural and forestry production, and solve human health and environmental problems.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Biologists, Botanists, Zoologists and Related Professionals** (2131): Biologists, Botanists, Zoologists and related professionals study living organisms and their interactions with each other and with the environment, and apply this knowledge to solve human health and environmental problems. They work in diverse fields such as botany, zoology, ecology, marine biology, genetics, immunology, pharmacology, toxicology, physiology, bacteriology and virology.
- **Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisors** (2132): Farming, Forestry and Fisheries Advisers study and provide assistance and advice on farm, forestry and fisheries management, including cultivation, fertilization, harvesting, soil erosion and composition, disease prevention, nutrition, crop rotation and marketing. They develop techniques for increasing productivity, and study and develop plans and policies for land and fisheries management.
- **Environmental Protection Professionals** (2133): Environmental Protection Professionals study and assess the effects of human activity on environment such as air, water and noise pollution, soil contamination, climate change, toxic waste and depletion and degradation of natural resources. They develop plans and solutions to protect, conserve, restore, minimize and prevent further damage to the environment.
