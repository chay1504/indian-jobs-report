# Medical and Pharmaceutical Technicians

NCO-2015 group 321. Category: Healthcare.

## What they do

Medical and Pharmaceutical Technicians perform technical tasks to assist in diagnosis and treatment of illness, disease, injuries and impairments.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians** (3211): Medical Imaging and Therapeutic Equipment Technicians test and operate radiographic, ultrasound and other medical imaging equipment to produce images of body structures for the diagnosis and treatment of injury, disease and other impairments. They may administer radiation treatments to patients under the supervision of a radiologist or other health professionals.
- **Medical and Pathology Laboratory Technicians** (3212): Medical and Pathology Laboratory Technicians perform clinical tests on specimens of bodily fluids and tissues in order to obtain information about the health of a patient or cause of death.
- **Pharmaceutical Technicians and Assistants** (3213): Pharmaceutical Technicians and Assistants perform a variety of tasks associated with dispensing medicinal products under the guidance of a pharmacist or other health professional.
- **Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians** (3214): Medical and Dental Prosthetic Technicians design, fit, service and repair medical and dental devices and appliances following prescriptions or instructions established by a health professional.
