# Secondary Education Teachers

NCO-2015 group 233. Category: Education.

## What they do

Secondary Education Teachers teach one or more subjects at secondary education level, excluding subjects intended to prepare students for employment in specific occupational areas.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Secondary Education Teachers** (2330): Secondary Education Teachers teach one or more subjects at secondary education level, excluding subjects intended to prepare students for employment in specific occupational areas.
