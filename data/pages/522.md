# Shop Salespersons

NCO-2015 group 522. Category: Sales.

## What they do

Shop Salespersons sell a range of goods and services directly to the public or on behalf of retail and wholesale establishments. They explain functions and qualities of these goods and services, and may operate small shops or supervise the activities of Shop Sales Assistants and cashiers.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Shopkeepers** (5221): Shopkeepers operate small retail shops either independently or with support from a small number of others.
- **Shop Supervisors** (5222): Shop Supervisors supervise and co-ordinate the activities of shop Sales Assistants, Checkout Operators and other Workers in retail and wholesale shops such as supermarkets and department stores.
- **Shop Sales Assistants** (5223): Shop Sales Assistants sell a range of goods and services directly to the public or on behalf of retail and wholesale establishments, and explain the functions and qualities of these goods and services.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Sales Supervisor, Wholesale Trade, Area Parts Manager (APM), Sales Supervisor, Retail Trade/CRM Manager, Shop Assistant, Order Processor, Jewellery Retail Sales Associate (Basic), Jewellery Retail Sales Associate (Advanced), Plumbing Products Sales Assistant, Trainee Associate

- Workers: about 4.30 crore (PLFS sample of 44,120 people)
- Employment type: 76% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 23% regular salaried, 1% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 2%
- Government/public sector employer: 0%
- Women: 22%; urban: 49%
- Typical education (median worker): Secondary (Class 10); graduate or above: 20%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Retail trade, Wholesale trade, Food & beverage service
