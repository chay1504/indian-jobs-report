# Waiters and Bartenders

NCO-2015 group 513. Category: Personal services.

## What they do

Waiters and Bartenders serve food and beverages in commercial dining and drinking places, clubs, institutions and canteens, on board ships and on passenger trains.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Waiters** (5131): Waiters serve food and beverages at tables in dining and drinking places, clubs, institutions and canteens, on board ships and on passenger trains.
- **Bartenders** (5132): Bartenders prepare, mix and serve alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks directly to customers over a bar or counter, or through waiters.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Butler, Steward, Hotel, Bearer, Domestic/Household Workers, Waiter, Institutional/Food and Beverage Service-Steward, Wine Waiter, Waiters, Other, Barman, Bartenders, Other
