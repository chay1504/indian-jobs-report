# General Office Clerks

NCO-2015 group 411. Category: Office & admin support.

## What they do

General Office Clerks perform a range of clerical and administrative tasks according to established procedures.

## Occupation families in this group

- **General Office Clerks** (4110): General Office Clerks perform a range of clerical and administrative tasks according to established procedures.
