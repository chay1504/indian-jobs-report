# Protective Service Workers

NCO-2015 group 541. Category: Protective services.

## What they do

Protective services workers protect individuals and property against fire and other hazards, maintain law and order and enforce laws and regulations.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Fire-fighters** (5411): Fire-fighters prevent, fight and extinguish fires and assist in other emergencies, protect life and property and conduct rescue efforts.
- **Police Officers** (5412): Police officers maintain law and order, patrolling public areas, enforcing laws and regulations and arresting suspected offenders.
- **Prison Guards** (5413): Prison guards watch over and maintain order among inmates of prisons, reformatories or penitentiaries.
- **Security Guards** (5414): Security guards patrol or monitor premises to guard property against theft and vandalism. They control access to establishments and maintain order and enforce regulations at public events and within establishments.
- **Protective Services Workers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (5419): This family covers protective services workers not classified elsewhere in the group 541, Protective services workers. For instance, this family includes lifeguards, crossing guards and animal control officers.

- Workers: about 65.0 lakh (PLFS sample of 5,945 people)
- Employment type: 0% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 99% regular salaried, 1% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 59%
- Government/public sector employer: 46%
- Women: 7%; urban: 58%
- Typical education (median worker): Secondary (Class 10); graduate or above: 23%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 192,000
- Main industries: Public administration & defence, Security & investigation, Households as employers of domestic workers
