# Legal, Social and Religious Associate Professionals

NCO-2015 group 341. Category: Legal, social, arts & media.

## What they do

Legal, social and religious associate professionals provide technical and practical services and support functions in legal processes and investigations, social and community assistance programmes and religious activities.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Legal and Related Associate Professionals** (3411): Legal and related associate professionals perform support functions in courts of law or in law offices, provide services related to such legal matters as insurance contracts, the transferring of property and the granting of loans and other financial transactions or conduct investigations for clients.
- **Social Work Associate Professionals** (3412): Social work associate professionals administer and implement social assistance programmes and community services and assist clients to deal with personal and social problems.
- **Religious Associate Professionals** (3413): Religious associate professionals provide support to ministers of religion or to a religious community, undertake religious works, preach and propagate the teachings of a particular religion and endeavour to improve well-being through the power of faith and spiritual advice.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Legal Assistant, Shirestedar, Petition Writer, Legal and Related Associate Professionals, Other, Blood Donor Unit Assistant, Equal Opportunity Officer, Social Work Associate Professionals
