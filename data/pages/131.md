# Production Managers in Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries

NCO-2015 group 131. Category: Management.

## What they do

Production Managers in Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries plan, direct, and co-ordinate production in large scale agricultural, horticultural, forestry aquaculture and fishery operations such as plantations, large ranches, collective farms and co-operatives to grow and harvest crops, breed and raise livestock, fish and shellfish and to catch and harvest fish and other forms of aquatic life.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers** (1311): Agricultural and Forestry Production Managers plan, direct, and co-ordinate production in large scale agricultural, horticultural and forestry operations such as plantations, large ranches, collective farms and agricultural co-operatives to grow and harvest crops, and breed and raise livestock.
- **Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers** (1312): Aquaculture and Fisheries Production Managers plan, direct, and co-ordinate production in large scale aquaculture and fishery operations to catch and harvest fish and shellfish, and to grow fish, shellfish or other forms of aquatic life, as cash crops or for release into freshwater or saltwater.
