# Child Care Workers and Teachers' Aides

NCO-2015 group 531. Category: Healthcare.

## What they do

Child Care Workers and Teachers' Aides provide care and supervision for children in schools, residential homes and child care facilities.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Child Care Workers** (5311): Child-care workers provide care and supervision for children in residential homes and in before-school, after-school, vacation and day care centres.
- **Teachers' Aides** (5312): Teachers' aides perform non-teaching duties to assist teaching staff, and provide care and supervision for children in schools and pre-schools.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Ayah, Institutional, Child Care Worker, Governess, Ayah, Domestic, Child Care Workers, Other

- Workers: about 10.1 lakh (PLFS sample of 973 people)
- Employment type: 4% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 92% regular salaried, 4% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 26%
- Government/public sector employer: 53%
- Women: 95%; urban: 46%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 12%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 96,000
- Main industries: Education, Social work, Other personal services (laundry, beauty, funeral)
