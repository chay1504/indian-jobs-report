# Artistic, Cultural and Culinary Associate Professionals

NCO-2015 group 343. Category: Legal, social, arts & media.

## What they do

Artistic, cultural and culinary associate professionals combine creative and technical skills to produce and maintain visual and audio images, and to prepare and plan meals: photographers, interior designers and decorators, gallery and museum technicians, chefs, and other artistic and cultural associate professionals.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Photographers** (3431): Photographers operate still cameras to photograph people, events, scenes, materials, products and other subjects.
- **Interior designers and decorators** (3432): Interior designers and decorators plan and design commercial, industrial, public, retail and residential building interiors to produce an environment tailored to a purpose, taking into consideration factors that enhance living and working environments and sales promotion. They co-ordinate and participate in their construction and decoration.
- **Gallery, Museum and Library Technicians** (3433): Gallery, museum and Library Technicians prepare artworks, specimens and artefacts for collections, arrange and construct gallery exhibits, and assist Librarians to organize and operate systems for handling recorded material and files.
