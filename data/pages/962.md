# Other Elementary Workers

NCO-2015 group 962. Category: Cleaners, helpers & elementary.

## What they do

Other Elementary Workers deliver and carry messages and packages, collect money and stock vending machines, read meters, collect water and firewood, collect and issue tickets for parking or events.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Messengers, Package Deliverers and Luggage Porters** (9621): Messengers, Package Deliverers and Luggage Porters carry and deliver messages, packages and other items within an establishment or between establishments, to households and elsewhere, or carry luggage especially at hotels, stations and airports.
- **Odd-job Persons** (9622): Odd job Persons clean, paint and maintain buildings, grounds and facilities, and undertake simple repairs.
- **Meter Readers and Vending Machine Collectors** (9623): Meter Readers and Vending-Machine Collectors stock vending machines and collect money from them or from parking meters and other coin-boxes, or read electricity, gas or water meters.
- **Water and Firewood Collectors** (9624): Water and firewood collectors collect water and firewood and transport them on foot or using hand or animal carts.
- **Elementary Workers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (9629): This family covers Elementary Workers not classified elsewhere in Division 9, Elementary workers. For instance, the group includes those who issue and collect parking or admission tickets, provide personal items to patrons or customers in cloak.
