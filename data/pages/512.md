# Cooks

NCO-2015 group 512. Category: Personal services.

## What they do

Cooks plan, organize, prepare and cook meals in hotels, restaurants and other eating places, on board ships, on passenger trains and in private households

## Occupation families in this group

- **Cooks** (5120): Cooks plan, organize, prepare and cook meals in hotels, restaurants and other eating places, on board ships, on passenger trains and in private households.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Cook, Institutional, Cook, Domestic, Cook, Ship, Kitchen Porter/Steward, Pantry Man, Cooks, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 67.5 lakh (PLFS sample of 5,250 people)
- Employment type: 26% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 66% regular salaried, 8% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 8%
- Government/public sector employer: 21%
- Women: 52%; urban: 48%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 5%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 120,000
- Main industries: Food & beverage service, Education, Households as employers of domestic workers
