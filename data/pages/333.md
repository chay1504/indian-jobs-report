# Business Service Agents

NCO-2015 group 333. Category: Business & finance.

## What they do

Business service agents establish contacts to sell various business services such as advertising space in the media, carry out customs clearance activities ensuring that necessary documents are in order, match jobseekers with vacancies, find workers for employers, arrange contracts for the performance of athletes, entertainers and artists, as well as for the publication of books, the production of plays, or the recording, performing and sale of music, plan and organize conferences and similar events.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Clearing and Forwarding Agents** (3331): Clearing and forwarding agents carry out customs clearing procedures and ensure that insurance, export/import licences and other formalities are in order.
- **Conference and Event Planners** (3332): Conference and event planners organize and co-ordinate services for conferences, events, functions, banquets and seminars.
- **Employment Agents and Contractors** (3333): Employment agents and contractors match jobseekers with vacancies, find workers for employers and contract labour for particular projects at the request of enterprises and other organizations including government, institutions, or find places for jobseekers for a commission.
- **Real Estate Agents and Property Managers** (3334): Real Estate Agents and Property Managers arrange the sale, purchase, rental and lease of real property, usually on behalf of clients and on a commission basis.
- **Business Service Agents, Not Elsewhere Classified** (3339): This family covers business services agents not classified elsewhere. ## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 10.7 lakh (PLFS sample of 1,032 people)
- Employment type: 78% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 22% regular salaried, 0% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 12%
- Government/public sector employer: 1%
- Women: 8%; urban: 71%
- Typical education (median worker): Diploma/certificate; graduate or above: 49%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 324,000
- Main industries: Real estate, Office admin & business support (incl. BPO/call centres), Auxiliary financial services
