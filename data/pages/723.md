# Machinery Mechanics and Repairers

NCO-2015 group 723. Category: Metal, machinery & electrical trades.

## What they do

Machinery mechanics and repairers fit, install, maintain and repair engines, vehicles, agricultural or industrial machinery and similar mechanical equipment.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Motor Vehicle Mechanics and Repairers** (7231): Motor vehicle mechanics and repairers fit, install, maintain, service and repair engines and the mechanical and related equipment of passenger cars, delivery trucks, motorcycles and other motor vehicles.
- **Aircraft Engine Mechanics and Repairers** (7232): Aircraft engine repairers and mechanics fit, service, repair and overhaul aircraft engines and assemblies, such as airframes, hydraulic, and pneumatic systems.
- **Agricultural and Industrial Machinery Mechanics and Repairers** (7233): Agricultural and industrial machinery mechanics and repairers fit, install, examine, service and repair engines, agricultural and industrial machinery and mechanical equipment, except motor vehicle, aircraft and electric motors.
- **Bicycle and Related Repairers** (7234): Bicycle and related repairers fit, maintain, service and repair the mechanical and related equipment of bicycles, rickshaws, baby carriages, wheelchairs and similar non-motorized transport equipment.

- Workers: about 61.5 lakh (PLFS sample of 5,607 people)

- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 14%
- Government/public sector employer: 4%
- Women: 4%; urban: 48%
- Typical education (median worker): Secondary (Class 10); graduate or above: 8%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Motor vehicle trade & repair, Repair & installation of machinery, Repair of computers & household goods
