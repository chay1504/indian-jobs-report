# Subsistence Crop Farmers

NCO-2015 group 631. Category: Agriculture, forestry & fishing.

## What they do

Subsistence crop farmers grow and harvest field or tree and shrub crops, vegetables and fruit, in order to provide food, shelter and a minimum of cash income for themselves and their households.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Subsistence Crop Farmers** (6310): Subsistence crop farmers grow and harvest field or tree and shrub crops, vegetables and fruit, in order to provide food, shelter and a minimum of cash income for themselves and their households.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)
