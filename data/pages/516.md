# Other Personal Services Workers

NCO-2015 group 516. Category: Personal services.

## What they do

Other Personal Services Workers recount past and predict future events in persons’ lives, provide companionship and other personal services, groom, train and care for animals, provide embalming and funeral services train people to drive vehicles.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Astrologers, Fortune Tellers and Related Workers** (5161): Astrologers, Fortune-Tellers and Related Workers recount past and predict future events in persons’ lives by practicing astrology, on the basis of characteristics of the clients’ palms, samples of playing cards drawn or other techniques.
- **Companions and Valets** (5162): Companions and Valets provide companionship and attend to various needs of the employer.
- **Undertakers and Embalmers** (5163): Undertakers and Embalmers perform various tasks in the disposal of dead human bodies.
- **Pet Groomers and Animal Care Workers** (5164): Pet Groomers and Animal Care Workers feed, handle, train and groom animals and assist Veterinarians, Animal Health Technologists and Technicians in maintaining veterinary facilities, animal shelters, breeding and boarding kennels, zoos, laboratories, retail pet shops, riding schools, dog training schools pet grooming and similar establishments.
- **Driving Instructors** (5165): Driving Instructors teach people how to drive motor vehicles.
- **Personal Service Workers, Not Elsewhere Classified** (5169): This family covers Personal Service Workers not classified elsewhere in Sub-Division 51, Personal Service Workers. For instance, those who provide services and companionship as dancing partners, social escorts, and night-club hostesses or hosts are classified here.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Astrologer, Palmist, Astrologers, Fortune Tellers and Related Workers, Other, Undertaker, Embalmer, Grave Digger, Undertakers and Embalmers, Other, Commercial Vehicle Driver Trainer

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 6.0 lakh (PLFS sample of 522 people)
- Employment type: 45% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 50% regular salaried, 5% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 12%
- Government/public sector employer: 14%
- Women: 27%; urban: 57%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 19%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 144,000
- Main industries: Other personal services (laundry, beauty, funeral), Public administration & defence, Education
