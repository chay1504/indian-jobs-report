# Street and Related Service Workers

NCO-2015 group 951. Category: Sales.

## What they do

Street and related service workers provide a variety of services on streets and in other public places, including cleaning shoes, washing car windows, running errands, looking after property, and providing other on-the-spot street services.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Street and Related Service Workers** (9510): Street and Related Service Workers provide a variety of services on streets and in other public places, including cleaning shoes, washing car windows, running errands, handing out leaflets, looking after property, and providing other services on-the-spot.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Boot Polisher, Bill Poster, Shoe Cleaning and Other Street Services Elementary Occupations, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 3.8 lakh (PLFS sample of 315 people)
- Employment type: 63% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 14% regular salaried, 23% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 5%
- Government/public sector employer: 27%
- Women: 28%; urban: 46%
- Typical education (median worker): Primary; graduate or above: 5%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 144,000
- Main industries: Retail trade, Civil engineering, Food & beverage service
