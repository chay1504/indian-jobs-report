# Creative and Performing Artists

NCO-2015 group 265. Category: Legal, social, arts & media.

## What they do

Creative and Performing Artists communicate ideas, impressions and facts in a wide range of media to achieve particular effects; interpret a composition such as a musical score or a script to perform or direct the performance; and host the presentation of such performance and other media events.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Visual Artists** (2651): Visual Artists create and execute works of art by sculpting, painting, drawing, creating cartoons, engraving or using other techniques.
- **Musicians, Singers and Composers** (2652): Musicians, singers and composers write, arrange, conduct and perform musical compositions.
- **Dancers and Choreographers** (2653): Dancers and choreographers conceive and create or perform dances.
- **Film, Stage and Related Directors and Producers** (2654): Film, Stage and Related Directors and Producers oversee and control the technical and artistic aspects of motion pictures, television or radio productions and stage shows.
- **Actors** (2655): Actors portray roles in motion pictures, television or radio productions and stage shows.
- **Announcers on Radio, Television and Other Media** (2656): Announcers on Radio, Television and Other media read news bulletins, conduct interviews, and make other announcements or introductions on radio, television, and in theatres and other establishments or media.
- **Creative and Performing Artists, Not Elsewhere Classified** (2659): This unit group covers all creative and performing artists not classified elsewhere. For instance, the group includes clowns, magicians, acrobats and other performing artists.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 8.2 lakh (PLFS sample of 689 people)
- Employment type: 78% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 17% regular salaried, 5% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 3%
- Government/public sector employer: 1%
- Women: 19%; urban: 58%
- Typical education (median worker): Secondary (Class 10); graduate or above: 22%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Arts & entertainment, Film, video, TV & music, Non-metallic mineral products (bricks, cement, glass)
