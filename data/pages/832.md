# Car, Van and Motorcycle Drivers

NCO-2015 group 832. Category: Drivers & transport.

## What they do

Car, Van and Motorcycle Drivers drive and tend motor cycles, motorized tricycles, cars or vans to transport passengers, materials or goods.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Motorcycle Drivers** (8321): Motor-Cycle Drivers drive and tend motor cycles or motorised tricycles equipped to transport materials, goods or passengers.
- **Car, Taxi and Van Drivers** (8322): Car, taxi and van drivers drive and tend motor cars and vans to transport passengers, mail or goods.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Driver Auto Rickshaw, Despatch Rider, Delivery Associate/Two Wheeler Delivery Associate, Motorcycle Drivers, Other, Driver, Car, Chauffer, Driver Personal, Chauffer, Cab Driver, Driver, Taxi, Driver, Ambulance, Light Motor Vehicle Driver, Car, Taxi and Van Drivers, Other

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 1.77 crore (PLFS sample of 14,917 people)
- Employment type: 67% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 27% regular salaried, 6% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 4%
- Government/public sector employer: 2%
- Women: 3%; urban: 46%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 6%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Land transport, Households as employers of domestic workers, Education
