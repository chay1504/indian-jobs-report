# Hairdressers, Beauticians and Related Workers

NCO-2015 group 514. Category: Personal services.

## What they do

Hairdressers, Beauticians and Related Workers cut and dress hair, shave and trim beards, give beauty treatment, apply cosmetics and make-up and give other kinds of treatment to individuals in order to improve their appearance.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Hairdressers** (5141): Hairdressers cut, style, colour, straighten and permanently wave hair, shave or trim facial hair and treat scalp conditions.
- **Beauticians and Related Workers** (5142): Beauticians and Related Workers, give facial and body beauty treatments, apply cosmetics and make-up and give other kinds of treatment to individuals in order to improve their appearance.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Hair Dresser (Ladies), Assistant Hair Stylist, Hair Stylist, Barber, Hairdressers, Other, Beautician, Assistant Beautician, Manicurist, Pedicurist and Manicurist, Skin Care Specialist, Make-Up-Man, Dresser, Stage and Studio, Wig Man, Bath Attendant, Beauticians and Related Workers, Others
