# Subsistence Livestock Farmers

NCO-2015 group 632. Category: Agriculture, forestry & fishing.

## What they do

Subsistence livestock farmers breed, raise and tend livestock in order to provide food, shelter and a minimum of cash income for themselves and their households.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Subsistence Livestock Farmers** (6320): Subsistence livestock farmers breed, raise and tend livestock in order to provide food, shelter and a minimum of cash income for themselves and their households.
