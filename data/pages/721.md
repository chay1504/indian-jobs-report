# Sheet and Structural Metal Workers, Moulders and Welders, and Related Workers

NCO-2015 group 721. Category: Metal, machinery & electrical trades.

## What they do

Sheet and structural metal workers, moulders and welders, and related workers, make moulds and cores for casting metal, weld and cut metal parts, make and repair articles of sheet metal, and install, erect, maintain and repair heavy metal structures, tackle, cable-cars and related equipment.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Metal Moulders and Core Makers** (7211): Metal Moulders and Coremakers make moulds and cores for casting metal.
- **Welders and Flame Cutters** (7212): Welders and flame cutters weld and cut metal parts using gas flame, electric arc and other sources of heat to melt and cut, or to melt and fuse metal.
- **Sheet Metal Workers** (7213): Sheet metal workers make, install and repair articles and parts of articles made out of sheet metal such as sheet steel, copper, tin, brass, aluminium, zinc or galvanised iron.
- **Structural Metal Preparers and Erectors** (7214): Structural metal preparers and erectors assemble, erect and dismantle structural metal frames of buildings and other structures.
- **Riggers and Cable Splicers** (7215): Riggers and cable splicers assemble rigging gear to move and position equipment and structural components, or install and maintain cables, ropes and wires on construction sites, buildings or other structures.

## India: who does this work (PLFS 2025, workers aged 15+)

- Workers: about 36.3 lakh (PLFS sample of 3,012 people)
- Employment type: 31% self-employed (incl. unpaid family helpers), 51% regular salaried, 18% casual labour
- Formal jobs (regular salaried with any social security): 16%
- Government/public sector employer: 3%
- Women: 3%; urban: 45%
- Typical education (median worker): Middle (Class 8); graduate or above: 6%
- Median annual earnings: Rs 180,000
- Main industries: Fabricated metal products, Basic metals, Repair & installation of machinery
