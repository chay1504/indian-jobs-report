# Financial and Mathematical Associate Professionals

NCO-2015 group 331. Category: Business & finance.

## What they do

Financial and Mathematical Associate professionals place a value on various items and property, maintain records of financial transactions, analyse loan application information and render a decision, buy and sell financial instruments, and perform mathematical and related calculations.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers** (3311): Securities and Finance Dealers and Brokers buy and sell securities, stocks, bonds and other financial instruments, and deal on the foreign exchange, on spot, or on futures markets, on behalf of their own company or for customers on a commission basis. They recommend transactions to clients or senior management.
- **Credit and Loan Officers** (3312): Credit and Loan Officers analyse and evaluate financial information on applications for credit and loans and determine approval or disapproval of the client for the credit or loan or recommend to management approval or disapproval.
- **Accounting Associate Professionals** (3313): Accounting Associate Professionals maintain complete records of financial transactions of an undertaking and verify accuracy of documents and records relating to such transactions.
- **Statistical, Mathematical and Related Associate Professionals** (3314): Statistical, Mathematical and Actuarial Associate professionals assist in planning the collection, processing and presentation of statistical, mathematical or actuarial data and in carrying out these operations, usually working under the guidance of statisticians, mathematicians and actuaries.
- **Valuers and Loss Assessors** (3315): Valuers and Loss Assessors value property and various goods and assess losses covered by insurance policies.
