# Finance Professionals

NCO-2015 group 241. Category: Business & finance.

## What they do

Finance Professionals plan, develop, organize, administer, invest, manage and conduct quantitative analyses of either financial accounting systems or funds for individuals, establishments and public or private institutions.

## Occupation families in this group

- **Accountants** (2411): Accountants plan, organize and administer accounting systems for individuals and establishments. Some occupations classified here examine and analyse the accounting and financial records of individuals and establishments to ensure accuracy and compliance with established accounting standards and procedures.
- **Financial and Investment Advisors** (2412): Financial and Investment Advisers develop financial plans for individuals and organizations, and invest and manage funds on their behalf.
- **Financial Analysts** (2413): Financial Analysts conduct quantitative analyses of information affecting investment programmes of public or private institutions.

## Example job titles (NCO-2015)

Accountant, Associate-Transactional Finance and Accounting, Auditor, Associate-Finance and Accounting Complex, Cost Accountant, Financial Examiner, Financial Analyst, Budget Analyst, Risk Management Analyst, Agriculture Field Officer, Commission Agent, Financial and Investment Advisors, Other
